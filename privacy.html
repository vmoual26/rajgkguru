Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RAJ G.K. GURU (a service of Aldhru Academy) ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information when you use https://rajgkguru.aldhruacademy.com (the "Site").

1. Information we collect

  • Registration details: your name, mobile number, email address and the exam you are preparing for, given when you register (email OTP sign-in).
  • Practice data: the tests you attempt, your answers, scores, time taken, flagged questions, questions already seen, and streaks. This is stored against your account so we can show your dashboard and avoid repeating questions.
  • Purchase records: which test packs you have bought, the payment reference and amount. We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details — payments are processed by our payment partner (Razorpay).
  • Feedback you send: messages through the contact form and "report a mistake" submissions.
  • Technical data: standard browser data and cookies/local storage needed to keep you signed in and to save an unfinished test on your device.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and deliver the test series you chose.
  • To show your progress and personalise practice (for example, avoiding questions you have already seen).
  • To process payments, prevent misuse and provide support.
  • To improve question quality (for example, reviewing reported mistakes).
  • To contact you about your account or purchases. We do not send third-party advertising.

3. Where data is stored and who processes it

Your account and practice data are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Payments are handled by Razorpay. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers above, when required by law, or to protect our rights and users. Leaderboards, if introduced, will show only a display name you choose.

5. Retention and your choices

We keep your data while your account is active. You may ask us to correct or delete your account and practice data by emailing support@aldhruacademy.com; we will act on verified requests within a reasonable time, except where we must keep purchase records by law.

6. Security

Access to your data is restricted to your own signed-in account using security rules, and paid content is served only to accounts that have purchased it. No online service can be perfectly secure; please keep your email account safe.

7. Children

The Site is meant for exam aspirants. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian's guidance.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use after a change means you accept it.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: support@aldhruacademy.com.
