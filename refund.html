Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy applies to purchases of paid test packs on https://rajgkguru.aldhruacademy.com, operated by RAJ G.K. GURU (a service of Aldhru Academy).

1. Nature of the product

Our packs are digital, online-only content. Access is enabled on your account immediately after successful payment; nothing is shipped.

2. Free trial first

Every exam has a free section (free tests and practice questions). Please try it before buying to check that the content suits you.

3. When we refund

  • Duplicate or failed-but-debited payment: refunded in full to the original payment method.
  • Technical problem: if you paid but could not access the pack and we are unable to fix it within 7 days of your report, we refund in full.
  • Change of mind: requests made within 24 hours of purchase, before you have attempted any paid test, are refunded in full.

4. When we do not refund

After you have started a paid test or viewed paid content beyond the 24-hour window, because the digital content has then been delivered and used.

5. How to request

Email support@aldhruacademy.com from your registered email address with your name, the exam pack and the payment reference. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 working days (bank processing times may add a few days).

6. Cancellation

There are no recurring charges. A pack is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to cancel after payment.
